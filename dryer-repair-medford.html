Common dryer issues in Medford homes

Dryer not heating

If the dryer runs but does not heat, we can inspect the heating system and related safety components.

Taking too long to dry

Long dry times can be caused by airflow problems, heating issues, or worn components.

Making noise

Scraping, squeaking, thumping, or grinding sounds can point to worn moving parts inside the dryer.
