Parts, cancellation, and rescheduling

Clear policies help us keep pricing simple and avoid confusion before the visit.

Do you install customer-provided parts?
No. Dryer Dudes does not install customer-provided parts. To protect the repair, warranty, and diagnosis process, any parts installed during a Dryer Dudes repair must be supplied through Dryer Dudes.
Why don’t you install parts I already bought?
Dryer parts can look similar but fail differently, and incorrect or low-quality parts can create repeat problems. We only warranty the parts we provide and the labor we perform. Customer-provided parts are not eligible for Dryer Dudes installation or warranty coverage.
What is your cancellation policy?
Appointments cancelled more than 48 hours before service may be eligible for refund back to the original payment method. Appointments cancelled within 48 hours of service are non-refundable.
Can I reschedule within 48 hours?
Appointments cannot be rescheduled within 48 hours of service. If you cannot be home, you may be able to switch to Authorized Entry instead so the appointment can still happen.
What is Authorized Entry?
Authorized Entry allows the technician to complete the dryer repair while you are not home, as long as the dryer is accessible and clear access instructions are provided. This can be a good option if you are inside the 48-hour window and cannot be home.
How do I cancel, ask a question, or switch to Authorized Entry?
Use Appointment Help with your Dryer Dudes job number. From there, you can ask questions, review cancellation options, or authorize entry.

Repair warranty

Dryer Dudes provides a 1-year limited repair warranty on the same repaired issue.

Do you warranty your repair?
Yes. Dryer Dudes provides a 1-year limited repair warranty on the same issue we repaired. If a covered issue returns within 1 year, Dryer Dudes may, at its discretion, redo the covered repair, replace a covered part, or refund the amount paid for the original covered repair. A refund resolves and ends that warranty claim.
What does the warranty cover?
The warranty applies only to the same repaired issue and the covered work or parts supplied by Dryer Dudes. Dryer Dudes chooses the remedy for a covered claim: redo the covered repair, replace a covered part, or refund the amount paid for the original covered repair.
What is not covered?
New or unrelated dryer problems are not covered by the original repair warranty. Issues caused by a different failed part, home electrical problems, venting or airflow outside the dryer, misuse, damage after the repair, blocked access, washers, stacked laundry units, or all-in-one washer-dryer combos are treated as a new service visit.
What if it turns out to be a different issue?
If the technician determines the problem is different from the original repaired issue, the customer is responsible for the service visit and any needed parts.
How do I request warranty service?
Use Appointment Help with your Dryer Dudes job number. This lets us confirm the original job before reviewing the warranty request.
