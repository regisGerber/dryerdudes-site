We do not service washers, stacked units, or all-in-one washer-dryer combos. Keeping the scope focused helps avoid confusion before you book.

Common dryer problems we help with

Dryer not heating

Heat issues are one of the most common reasons customers book dryer repair.

Dryer not starting

If the dryer will not start, we can inspect the common failure points and repair what is needed.

Long dry times

Taking multiple loads or taking too long to dry can point to airflow, heating, or component issues.

Noisy dryer

Grinding, squeaking, thumping, or scraping sounds can come from worn moving parts inside the dryer.
